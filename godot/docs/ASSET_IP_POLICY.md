# Rush Track — Asset & IP Policy

## Scope
Rush Track is an original 2D hill-racing game targeted only at Android devices.

## Asset rules
- Use original SVG/PNG/texture assets made specifically for Rush Track, or assets with a clearly documented license compatible with commercial distribution.
- Do not copy, trace, rip, extract, or recreate distinctive protected game assets, logos, characters, UI, sounds, maps, or vehicles from existing games.
- Do not use third-party brand logos, real vehicle trademarks, celebrity likenesses, or branded environments in shipped art.
- Keep original source files for authored assets where practical.
- Record any third-party dependency and its license before it enters the project.

## Current authored assets
The lobby backdrop, player buggy, and Rush Track mark in this branch are original SVG artwork created for this project.

## Legal note
This policy is a development safeguard, not legal advice or a guarantee of worldwide copyright/trademark clearance. Before commercial release, perform a final jurisdiction-specific IP review of the complete shipped build, store listing, fonts, audio, code dependencies, and branding.
