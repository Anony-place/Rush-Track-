## What we do NOT collect

Rush Track is a local-first, single-player game. We do **not** collect,
store, or transmit:

- your name, email address, or any account identifiers,
- your device location, contacts, photos, or media,
- any gameplay data, scores, or progress (your save file lives only on your
  device and never leaves it).

## What we share with service providers

### Advertising (when you have not purchased Remove Ads)

We show ads through **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, Google may
receive the following (as required by Google's policy): Android ID,
Advertising ID, app version, and device model/OS version. Google's privacy
practice: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### In-app purchases

When you buy coins or the Remove Ads pass, the purchase is processed by
**Google Play Billing**. Google receives the standard transaction data
(product ID, amount, transaction ID). We never see your payment details.
Google Play's privacy practice: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Device diagnostics (optional, if enabled by your device/OS)

Standard Android system logs that your device may send to Google as part of
the Android platform itself.

## Children

Rush Track is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Data retention & deletion

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to retain or delete.
Deleting the app removes your local save file from your device.

## Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the
"last updated" date. Material changes (new data collection) will also be
reflected in Play Console data safety declarations.
